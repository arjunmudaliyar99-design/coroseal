Reactor Vessels

Overview

Coroseal designs & manufactures FRP Reactor tanks in a wide range of shapes, sizes and configurations. Reactors can be fabricated with dish bottom, conical bottom, bolted flat top or closed top according to customer requirements.

Agitator Assemblies

Complete agitator assemblies including motor, gearbox, bearing housing and VFD panels can be supplied and mounted as required.

Materials of Construction

FRP / GRP using isophthalic or bisphenol vinyl ester resins; dual laminate or thermoplastic linings available.
