Storage Tanks

← Back to Products

Overview

Coroseal designs & manufactures FRP and dual-laminate tanks to handle corrosive chemicals in several shapes and sizes. Tanks are manufactured by hand lay-up and filament winding; capacities up to 150 m3 in-shop, larger tanks assembled on-site.

Types

  • Pressure Vessels
  • Storage Tanks
  • Pulp Storage Tanks
  • Electrolysis Tanks

Accessories

Flanged nozzles, threaded couplings, access ports, level gauges, internal baffles, agitator supports, ladders and handrails are available.
