Pipes & Fittings

Overview

Coroseal manufactures FRP pipes and fittings up to 3000 mm diameter and lengths up to 12,000 mm. Pipes and fittings can be hand lay-up or filament winding and are available with thermoplastic linings for chemical resistance.

Fittings Range

  • Elbows
  • Reducers (concentric & eccentric)
  • Flanges (full face & blind)
  • Tees, spool pieces, headers
